# Euvetia case study direction

## Goal
Continue the existing Euvetia case study page from the current stopping point in a way that feels premium, editorial, and highly readable for recruiters.

## What to preserve
- Existing top section and current visual language
- Spacious layout
- Bold display headings
- Serif body text
- Swiss-inspired restraint
- Soft neutral background
- Red accent moments
- Calm, high-trust tone
- Strong hierarchy and generous whitespace

## Reading experience
- Easy to scan
- Not too text-heavy
- Alternate between narrative sections and visual sections
- Use screenshots, stat moments, visual breaks, and process storytelling
- Make the page feel crafted, not generic
- Leave elegant blank/image placeholders when assets are missing

## Creative direction
Take loose inspiration from Apple, Spotify, and Jitter in pacing, polish, composition, and storytelling rhythm.
Do not imitate them literally.

## Implementation rules
- Keep content driven by YAML
- Do not hardcode large content blocks in the page component
- Reuse and extend section types only when necessary
- Prefer scalable section patterns over one-off custom sections
- Preserve consistency with the existing case study renderer
